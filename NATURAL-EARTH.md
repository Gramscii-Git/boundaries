# Natural Earth terms of use

`europe.geo.json` and `world.geo.json` are adapted from Natural Earth,
*Admin 0 – Countries*, 1:50m scale.

Natural Earth states that all versions of its raster and vector map data
are in the public domain: the data may be used, modified and distributed
in any manner, for any purpose, without permission and without
attribution. Natural Earth's own words are at
<https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/>.

Natural Earth is a public-domain map dataset built through a
collaboration of volunteers and supported by NACIS, the North American
Cartographic Information Society. Crediting it is welcome, not required.

The adaptation in this repository (projection, simplification, SVG paths
and the identifiers attached to each shape) is released by Gramscii under
CC BY 4.0, see `LICENSE`.
